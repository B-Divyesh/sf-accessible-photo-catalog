Terms

Large Type Catalog is a free local sorting aid. It prepares sidecar data; it does not modify, move, rename, or delete your original photos.

Your responsibility

Keep your own backups of important photos and exported catalog data. Review CSV contents before using them with another tool. A “Reject” decision is only a label in the catalog and is not a deletion command.
