Privacy

Your photos stay on your device. Large Type Catalog has no account, analytics, advertising, or upload service.

What is stored

When you choose a folder, the browser stores local copies of its image files and your decisions, tags, queued filenames, and notes in IndexedDB. Display preferences and the folder label are stored in local storage. This data stays inside this browser profile so the catalog survives a refresh and works offline.

What leaves your device

Nothing from your catalog is sent to us. The production host may keep standard short-lived server access logs needed to deliver the app, such as an IP address, requested page, and time. We do not add cookies or tracking scripts.

Your control

Use “Export CSV” for a readable sidecar or “Export backup” for reusable metadata. Use Display → Clear saved catalog to remove the browser copies and decisions. Clearing site data in browser settings does the same. Original files in your folder are never changed.
