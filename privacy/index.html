Privacy

Photos and catalog data stay in this browser. Large Type Catalog has no account, analytics, advertising, or upload service.

What is stored

When you choose a folder, IndexedDB stores local copies of its images and your catalog details. Those details include decisions, tags, queued filenames, and notes. Browser storage keeps your display choices and folder label after a reload. The catalog then survives a refresh and works offline.

What leaves your device

Loading this site sends standard page requests to the host. Your photos and catalog details are not included. We do not add cookies or tracking scripts.

Your control

Use “Export CSV” for one row per photo. Use “Export backup” for saved decisions, tags, notes, and filenames. In the catalog, choose “Adjust display,” then “Clear saved catalog.” It removes browser copies and decisions. Clearing this browser's site data does the same. Original files in your folder are never changed.
